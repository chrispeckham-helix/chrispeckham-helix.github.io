SmartDFE™ is an award-winning collection of innovative and market-proven components that enable your engineering team to get to market quickly whilst giving them the space and capability to add their own value. SmartDFE is a full software stack for high-speed, single-pass inkjet presses. It includes everything from job creation and pre-press workflow through to an AI-accelerated RIP that drives printhead drive electronics inline.
