Join Global Graphics Software at Label Forum Japan in Tokyo! On show will be [**SmartDFE**](https://www.globalgraphics.com/products/smartdfe/)™, the AI-accelerated inline Digital Front End for high-speed, single-pass label and packaging presses. An award-winning collection of innovative and market-proven components, SmartDFE enables your engineering team to get to market quickly and includes everything from job creation and pre-press workflow through to printhead drive electronics. Also on display will be a range of software solutions from HYBRID Software including enterprise production workflows, native PDF editors, and 3D rendering and visualization software for labels and packaging.
