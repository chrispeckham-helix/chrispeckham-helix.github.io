**The China International Exhibition on Label Printing Technology 2026** Join the team from Helix and colleagues from Hybrid Software at the 2026 South China International Label and Sino Pack exhibition. On show will be [**SmartDFE**](https://hybridhelix.com/products/smartdfe/)™, the AI-accelerated inline Digital Front End platform for high-speed, single-pass label and packaging presses. An award-winning collection of innovative and market-proven components, SmartDFE enables your engineering team to get to market quickly and includes everything from job creation and pre-press workflow through to printhead drive electronics. Also on display will be a range of software solutions from Hybrid Software including enterprise production workflows, native PDF editors, and 3D rendering and visualization software for labels and packaging. **For your personal demonstration contact: Bob Zhang, OEM Sales Manager, Asia Pacific email: bob.zhang@hybridsoftware.com or telephone: +86 13601858427** Based in Guangzhou and capitalizing South China’s strengths in label printing, the China International Exhibition on Label Printing Technology (also known as “Sino-Label”) has established itself as an influential international exhibition with steady growth every year.
