Join Global Graphics Software and our colleagues from Hybrid Software, Meteor Inkjet and Xitron at Labelexpo Europe, 16 - 19 September in Barcelona. See us in **Hall 5, Stand 5E55.** The team will be presenting demos of the next major version of [SmartDFE](https://www.globalgraphics.com/products/smartdfe/labels-and-packaging/)**™**, the AI-powered suite of intelligent components designed to optimize label and packaging press performance. Recently recognized with the King’s Award for Enterprise for Innovation, SmartDFE is a full software stack that provides everything you need to add labels and packaging printing to an industrial production environment. This major new version introduces new capabilities designed to empower your press operators when the press-press department is offline. [ARRANGE YOUR PERSONAL DEMO OF SMARTDFE](https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=r_2hK0Z6ekyoFT2ofBrFTQzv7aDnsvdBllwPPJhB6p9UREk1NDlQUk1RWEFLNUhUSFFaR0hVSVpRNS4u&origin=lprLink&route=shorturl) Hybrid Software will be showcasing its range of pre-press software and workflow tools and will debut PACKZ Max—the first-ever integration of the [Harlequin RIP](https://www.globalgraphics.com/products/harlequin/)® directly into a PDF-based prepress editor. PACKZ Max empowers prepress professionals with real-time RIP previews, precise screening control, and seamless 1-bit TIFF output, optimized for flexo and digital printing. This ensures unmatched image fidelity, color consistency, and production efficiency—right from file preparation through to plate creation. Labelexpo Europe is the largest event for the label and package printing industry with products, launches and live demonstrations. If you're an industry supplier, a printer, converter of labels, a brand owner or label and packaging designer, then the event is for you. We look forward to seeing you!
