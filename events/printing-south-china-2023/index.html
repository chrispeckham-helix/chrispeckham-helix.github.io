Join OEM sales manager Bob Zhang for his presentation at [Printing South China 2023](https://www.printingsouthchina.com/PRT23/idx/eng) at the Industry 4.0 - Digital Intelligent Prepress Forum: **Friday, 3rd March 2023** **A Smart Factory needs a Smart Digital Front End** Smart factories are designed to autonomously run the entire production process and this will include the print subsystems. However, adding print to these smart factories requires a rethink in the software and hardware stack. The Digital Front End (DFE) will also need to be smart and to get mass adoption the Smart DFE must be no more expensive than today's print systems and DFEs.
