Join us at the Industrial Print Integration Conference where industry partners and experts from all industrial print technologies come together to consult on best practices and new developments. **Don't miss Victor Asseiceiro: Advanced color management solutions for every print technology 12 November 2025 - Track 2 - 11:20 am** Hybrid Software General Manager of Color, Victor Asseiceiro, will speak about the modern approaches to color management system design for industrial applications, sharing insights on how print system manufacturers and software developers are leveraging component-based color technologies to meet demanding market requirements. Also attending are Justin Bailey from Global Graphics and Janis Katomeris from ColorLogic, offering expert insights into how Hybrid Software's modular, OEM-ready technologies are reshaping the print landscape—from packaging and labels to décor and textiles.
