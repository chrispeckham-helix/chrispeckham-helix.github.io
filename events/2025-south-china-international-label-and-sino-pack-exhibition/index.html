Join Global Graphics Software and colleagues from Hybrid Software at the 2025 South China International Label and Sino Pack exhibition. Visit us on stand 2D05 in the Smart Factory zone. An award-winning collection of innovative and market-proven components, SmartDFE enables your engineering team to get to market quickly and includes everything from job creation and pre-press workflow through to printhead drive electronics. Also on display will be a range of software solutions from HYBRID Software including enterprise production workflows, native PDF editors, and 3D rendering and visualization software for labels and packaging.
