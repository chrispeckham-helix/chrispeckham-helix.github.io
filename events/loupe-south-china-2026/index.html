LOUPE South China 2026 (formerly Labelexpo South China)

Start
Wed Dec 02 2026 12:20:00 GMT+0000 (Coordinated Universal Time)
End
Fri Dec 04 2026 12:20:00 GMT+0000 (Coordinated Universal Time)
Venue
Shenzen World Exhibition & Convention Centre, Shenzen
Join the team from Hybrid Software at LOUPE South China 2026 (formerly Labelexpo South China) where we'll be showcasing SmartDFE, the digital front end platform for high-speed, single-pass label and packaging presses along with our brand-new technology, SmartRIP, a GPU-native RIP (raster image processor) designed for industial inkjet applications. Also on show will be the latest innovations for labels and packaging, including the award-winning PACKZ PDF editor and CLOUDFLOW workflow automation software. LOUPE South China is the region's largest event for the label and packaging printing industry, attracting over 26,000 visitors from 93 countries. To arrange a meeting, contact: Bob Zhang - OEM Sales Manager, Asia Pacific on email: [bob.zhang@hybridsoftware.com](mailto:bob.zhang@hybridsoftware.com) [Find out more and register](https://www.loupe-global.com/loupe-south-china)
