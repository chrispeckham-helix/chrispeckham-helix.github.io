Join Global Graphics Software at Labelexpo Europe, 11 - 14 September in Brussels. We'll be on Stand 8B/45 alongside our colleagues from [Hybrid Software Group](https://www.hybridsoftware.group/). The team will be presenting demos of [SmartDFE](/products/smartdfe/)™, the only AI-powered Inline RIP with label and packaging print intelligence. It's a full software and hardware stack that provides everything you need to add labels and packaging printing to an industrial production environment. To arrange a demo or a chat simply email us: [sales@globalgraphics.com](mailto:sales@globalgraphics.com) Labelexpo Europe is the largest event for the label and package printing industry with products, launches and live demonstrations. If you're an industry supplier, a printer, converter of labels, a brand owner or label and packaging designer, then the event is for you. We look forward to seeing you!
