Print China 2023

Join Bob Zhang and colleagues from Hybrid Software Group at Print China 2023.

Join Bob Zhang and colleagues from Hybrid Software Group at Print China 2023. We'll be showcasing [SmartDFE](https://www.globalgraphics.com/products/smartdfe/complete)™, the complete software and hardware stack that adds print to the smart factory. Designed for high-speed, single-pass, label and packaging inkjet presses, SmartDFE includes the creation of optimized print-ready PDF files; workflow and job automation to connect with enterprise IT systems; RIPping; screening; optimized image quality; and via Harlequin Direct, it drives the print data directly to the printheads at blistering speeds through Meteor Inkjet's software and electronics. Contact Bob Zhang: [bob.zhang@globalgraphics.com](mailto:bob.zhang@globalgraphics.com) to arrange your personal demo. Experts from Hybrid Software Group will be available to discuss core printing technologies including color management, high-speed Digital Front Ends (DFEs) and RIPs, pre-press software for labels and packaging, and printhead drive electronics.
